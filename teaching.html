Presentations, Teaching and Outreach

My teaching connects observations of minerals, rocks, and landscapes to the processes that formed them. In introductory geology, I move between mineral textures and planetary evolution, using examples from field geology and research to make those connections concrete. My experience includes university lectures and laboratories, school outreach, teacher workshops, and explaining Grand Canyon geology to visitors.
