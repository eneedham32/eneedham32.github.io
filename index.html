Éamonn Needham

Postdoctoral Researcher

Earth and Planetary Sciences · University of California, Davis

I am a volcanologist, geochemist, and experimental petrologist at the University of California, Davis. I use experiments, microscopy, and geochemical measurements to study magmatic processes on Earth and the Moon.

My research focuses on volcanic fragmentation, mineral growth, diffusion, and the interpretation of geochemical records.
