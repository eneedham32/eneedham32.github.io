Research

My research treats physical and chemical heterogeneity as a fundamental feature of igneous systems. I combine experiments, imaging, and geochemistry to test the assumptions behind our methods and identify when simplification obscures the processes recorded in rocks.

Volcanology

Research overview ↑

Fragmentation and Scoria Formation at Tecolote

Micro-textural analysis of Tecolote scoria examines particle formation and the juxtaposition of brittle and ductile fragmentation products.

Cryptotephra in the Archaeological Record

Trace element methods source volcanic glass in archaeological records and test how heating can modify the geochemical signatures used for correlation.

Experimental Petrology

Research overview ↑

Quartz Growth and Titanium Uptake (TitaniQ)

Quartz growth experiments test titanium incorporation, the role of amorphous precursors, and the assumptions behind TitaniQ thermobarometry.

Quartz Growth, Fault Healing, and Tremor

Quartz growth experiments investigate the development of cohesion and its relationship to fault healing and tremor.

Geochemistry & Petrology

Research overview ↑

Early Lunar Magmas and Impact Modification

Al-in-zircon systematics test whether zircons and surrounding glass share a common history, revealing impact modification while preserving records of early lunar magmas.

Read publication

Ocellar Quartz and Magma Plumbing

Field observations and petrography connect ocellar quartz textures to crystal growth and processes within magma plumbing systems.

Modelling

Research overview ↑

Magma Storage Beneath Mt. Hood

Sr–Mg diffusion modelling, U–Th dating, and Ti-in-zircon thermometry reveal simultaneous warm and cold storage within a single magma reservoir.

Thermal–Chemical Diffusion at Pyramid Peak

Thermal–chemical models connect calcium isotope gradients, intrusion textures, and field observations to element transport and cooling.
